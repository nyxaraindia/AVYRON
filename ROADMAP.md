# AVYRON Research Roadmap

This is a flexible research programme, not a fixed deadline.

## Phase 0 — Scientific foundations and prior art
Study robotics, AI, mathematics, control theory, existing architectures,
programming languages, academic literature and patents.

## Phase 1 — Universal intelligence architecture
Define research questions, hypotheses, system boundaries,
capability representation and architecture.

## Phase 2 — AVYRON Language
Design philosophy, syntax, grammar, types, semantics,
constraints, task representation and error model.

## Phase 3 — Compiler and intermediate representation
Research lexing, parsing, AST, semantic analysis,
capability checking, verification, IR and backend translation.

## Phase 4 — AVYRON Runtime
Design execution, scheduling, monitoring, communication,
resource management and failure recovery.

## Phase 5 — Adaptive intelligence
Research perception, reasoning, planning, capability composition,
strategy selection and resource-aware computation.

## Phase 6 — Learning and safety
Design controlled learning, memory, skill validation,
versioning, regression testing, rollback and safety enforcement.

## Phase 7 — Multi-embodiment validation
Test across substantially different simulated robot bodies.
Compare against appropriate established baselines.

## Phase 8 — Advanced research
Investigate multi-robot cooperation, future embodiments,
scalability and formal verification.

Each phase requires evidence and review before major implementation.
